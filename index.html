FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is everything confidential?+

Yes. Everything you share stays strictly between us. Confidentiality is the foundation of every conversation we have.

Do I need a referral?+

No referral is needed. Anyone can book a session directly through the form, by phone, or by email.

Do you offer online sessions?+

Yes. Sessions are available both in person and online, so you can talk from wherever you feel most comfortable.

How long is a session?+

A typical session lasts about 50–60 minutes. We'll always go at a pace that feels right for you.

How do I book an appointment?+

Use the booking form below, call us, or send an email. We'll confirm a date and time that works for you.

What if I don't know what to talk about?+

That's completely okay. Many people start that way. We can begin wherever you are, and figure it out together.
